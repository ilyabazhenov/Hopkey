QKey → link

Got an issue number? Hopkey opens the link.

Copy text with a key, and a notification appears; click it to open the page. Or select a key in any app and press Q in the palette.

  • Regex → URL templates. Jira is just one preset, next to GitHub, GitLab, CVE and any pattern of your own.
  • Several keys in one text open together. Case doesn’t matter: proj-7 → PROJ-7.
  • Prefer no notification? Open links right away with one switch in Settings.

1–9Snippets

A meeting link or a canned reply, one digit away.

Predefined values (meeting links, canned replies, billing details, test-environment passwords) are pasted wherever your cursor is. Use a digit in the palette, or the full list with ⌃⌥V on top of any app.

  • Values live in the macOS Keychain as a single item, read once per launch, so you don’t get a string of access prompts.
  • Even a test password never lingers in clipboard history or other clipboard managers, and never travels to your iPhone. A copied one is wiped from the clipboard right after you paste it.
  • Pasting into another app needs Accessibility. Without it, the value simply lands in the clipboard.

Keep production passwords in your password manager. Hopkey is for test environments and the things you need many times a day.

MSound and mic

Your mic is muted before you even join the call.

Mute works system-wide through CoreAudio, in any calling app. Press M in the palette or ⇧⌘M from anywhere, and the menu bar icon shows whether anyone can hear you.

  • Switch audio output and input: headphones, speakers, a headset mic.
  • Volume levels on the “Sound” card, with arrow keys and no mouse.

Keyboard layouts

Any keyboard layout works.

Palette letters are matched by the physical key, not the character it types. M stays mute even when you’re typing in Cyrillic and that key produces “Ь”.

Privacy

Everything stays on your Mac.

Hopkey has no servers, no account and no telemetry. Snippets and clipboard history are never synced to iCloud or any other cloud.

Snippets in Keychain

Values live in the macOS Keychain, and other apps can’t read them without your permission. A password is wiped from the clipboard right after you paste it.

Encrypted clipboard

History is stored on disk with AES-256-GCM, the key kept in Keychain. Copies a password manager marks as concealed are never saved.

Notes are plain files

Ordinary unencrypted files in Application Support. Take them with you, no Hopkey needed, but keep passwords in snippets.

FAQ

What people ask first.

Why does Hopkey need Accessibility?

To put the selected text into the key input window and to paste a snippet into another app’s field, Hopkey presses ⌘C and ⌘V for you. Without it, the input window opens empty and a snippet goes to the clipboard. Mic mute, keep-awake and text from screen need no extra permissions.

Is it only for Jira?

No. Recognition is set up with regex → URL templates. There are Jira, GitHub, GitLab and CVE presets; just fill in your domain or repository. You can add any pattern of your own.

Can I use something other than double ⌘?

Yes. In Settings, the double tap can switch to ⌥ or be turned off. A left click on the menu bar icon also opens the palette, and frequent commands can get direct hotkeys.

Why doesn’t the key turn into a link right inside Slack or Telegram?

macOS doesn’t let one app insert links into another app’s windows, and the system’s Text Replacements only do static swaps. So Hopkey recognizes the key and opens the right page instead.

Will it run on my Mac?

You need macOS 13 or later. It’s a universal build that runs on both Apple Silicon and Intel. Updates arrive through Sparkle.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Hopkey is free and open source under the MIT license.
